#### <font id=6 color="#AAA">function</font> <font color="#44a8f2">DjiLiveview_StartH264Stream</font>

<table style="text-align:left;border:none">
    <tr>
      <td>功能：按选定位置启动 FPV 或摄像机 H264 流。</td>  
      <td>product：all</td>
    </tr>
</table>


```c
T_DjiReturnCode DjiLiveview_StartH264Stream(E_DjiLiveViewCameraPosition position, E_DjiLiveViewCameraSource source,
                                            DjiLiveview_H264Callback callback);
```

<font color="#AAA">参数</font>        
<br/><font color="#70BF41">position</font>：指出哪个相机输出H264流
<br/><font color="#70BF41">source</font>：指出哪个子摄像头输出H264码流   
<br/><font color="#70BF41">callback</font>：接收到新的h264帧时在回调线程中调用的回调函数     
<br/><font color="#AAA">返回值</font>
<br/>根据程序执行的情况输出对应的返回值，详情请参见：<a href="../core/dji-error.html">DjiErrorCode</a>
